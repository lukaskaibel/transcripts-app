Recording other people

You decide what the app records, and you are responsible for it. Before you record a conversation, tell the people in it, and ask for their consent wherever the law requires it. In Germany, for example, recording words that aren't spoken in public without consent is a criminal offence (§ 201 StGB), and other countries have similar rules. If you record at work, your organisation's rules apply too.

The app never starts a recording by itself. The reminder when a meeting begins and the notice when a call starts only ask.

Transcripts can be wrong

Transcripts, the names given to voices and the summaries are made automatically and can be wrong: a word misheard, a line given to the wrong person, a decision summed up badly. Check what matters before you rely on it or pass it on.
